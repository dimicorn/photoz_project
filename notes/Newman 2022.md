# Newman 2022

[источник] Jeffrey A. Newman, Daniel Gruen, «Photometric Redshifts for Next-Generation Surveys», Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics, т. 60, вып. 1, с. 363–414, 2022.
[источник] Аннотация: Photometric redshifts are essential in studies of both galaxy evolution and cosmology, as they enable analyses of objects too numerous or faint for spectroscopy. The Rubin Observatory, Euclid, and Roman Space Telescope will soon provide a new generation of imaging surveys with unprecedented area coverage, wavelength range, and depth.
[порождено] Зачем проекту: обзор требований к обучающим выборкам photo-z; на него опирается тезис о нерепрезентативности (Ashmead 2026, с. 2).
